# B-Atlas Production Validation Batch 12 — Atlantic + Atlantic Boat + BHM + Seaway + Sisu + True North + Windy + Luhrs + Gozzard + Transpacific Marine

**Release:** v6.42.0  
**Canonical architecture:** v1.4

## Atlantic
The Atlantic 37 Double Cabin is the direct successor to the Prairie 36 after Atlantic acquired Prairie's molds and tooling. It receives strong dimensional, keel, two-stateroom and dual-stall-shower facts.

## Atlantic Boat / Duffy 26
The Duffy 26 hull dates from 1986, not 1982. The same basic hull has been completed as commercial lobsterboat, launch, water taxi, tow boat and pleasure yacht. Stable hull identity is canonicalized while completion-specific weight, tankage and accommodations are deliberately not forced model-wide.

## BHM 32
The BHM/Flye Point 32 is identified as a Spencer Lincoln-designed Maine Downeast hull. Because individual Flye Point completions vary, B-Atlas keeps universal dimensions conservative instead of promoting one brokered boat's measurements as factory truth.

## Seaway
The 24/25 coastal family includes outboard and diesel inboard/I-O configurations. Fuel and propulsion therefore remain configuration-specific on the 24 Sport Trawler. The 28 Coastal Cruiser remains lower-confidence because a strong model-specific primary specification source was not found in this pass.

## Sisu
The Sisu 22 spans meaningful builder and propulsion evolution: early inboard diesels and later composite/outboard production. Model-wide fuel/propulsion are therefore unresolved.

The Sisu 26 has stronger published dimensions of 25'11" × 9'8" × 2'9", about 7,200 lb. Both gasoline and diesel direct-drive examples are documented.

## True North
The original diesel True North 34 is a separate generation from the later Catalina-era outboard boat. It began as the True North 33 in 2003; a transom modification later made it the 34. With 22-knot cruise and 25-knot top speeds, its hull behaviour is corrected from semi-displacement to planing-capable.

## Windy
Windy's own heritage page gives 7.75 m length, 2.93 m beam and 2,350 kg weight for the 26 Snekke. It does not publish draft. A surviving 1986 listing gives 1.01 m, while B-Atlas legacy data used about 0.72 m. Rather than choose one unsupported figure, draft is now unresolved.

## Luhrs
The Alura 30 is strongly documented as a semi-displacement, full-keel single-inboard cruiser/fishing boat. Its keel was redesigned in 1988 to reduce vibration, so the change is captured as a production phase.

## Gozzard / Pilgrim 40
The Pilgrim 40 is a full-displacement Canadian motor yacht designed by H. Ted Gozzard and built by North Castle Marine/Gozzard Yachts. Published specifications support 40' × 14' × 3'3", 22,000 lb and single 100 hp diesel propulsion.

## Transpacific Marine / Eagle 32
The Eagle 32 receives strong HMY specifications and is retained as a full-displacement pilothouse trawler with full protective keel, single diesel and separate stall shower.

## Architecture result
This batch reinforces the B-Atlas rule that semi-custom hulls should have stable hull facts separated from completion-specific equipment, while repeated model names and later generations must not overwrite older canonical identities.
