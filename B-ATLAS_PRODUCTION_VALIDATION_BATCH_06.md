# B-Atlas Production Validation Batch 06 — Ranger Tugs + Cutwater + C-Dory + American Tug + Rosborough

**Release:** v6.36.0  
**Canonical architecture:** v1.4  
**Scope:** 33 current B-Atlas records.

## Ranger Tugs

Ranger's own current and past-model archives provide a strong identity framework.

The most important current-generation correction is the **R-27**. The 2026 boat is twin Yamaha F150 outboard powered. That is materially different from the earlier single-outboard R-27 NW/LE, so B-Atlas retains the existing separate searchable records rather than flattening them into one universal engine specification.

Current R-23, R-27, R-29 S, R-31 S/CB and R-43 S/CB specifications were refreshed from factory material.

The R-43 records are now correctly classified with **twin Volvo IPS pod drives**, not conventional shafts.

## Cutwater

Cutwater remains closely related to Ranger Tugs at the manufacturer/company level but is retained as its own brand.

The C-28 receives stronger modified-V/trailerable specification data.

The C-30 Command Bridge and C-30 Sedan are explicitly a shared-hull configuration pair: the Command Bridge is the same basic boat with a retractable flybridge.

## C-Dory

The historical **26 Pro Angler is real**.

Owner-community evidence includes a 2005 C-Dory 26 Pro Angler with HIN `CD026021I405`, as well as another owner identifying a 1986 hull. This is enough to reverse the earlier B-Atlas suspicion that the model might be fictitious. Exact production range and configuration history remain uncertain, so those details are not over-normalized.

The TomCat 255 is normalized as a power catamaran.

The current 26 Venture receives manufacturer specifications including 25'9" LOA, 8'6" beam, 14" stated draft, 4,120 lb weight and composite-core/fiberglass construction.

## American Tug

American Tug's own history calls its founding design the **AT 34/365**.

B-Atlas therefore retains 34 and 365 as searchable marketplace designations while explicitly linking them as one core model lineage rather than pretending they are unrelated hulls.

Current factory specifications are added for the 365 and 395.

## Rosborough

The RF-246 Wheelhouse and Sedan Cruiser are configurations of the same all-fiberglass modified-V RF-246 hull.

Historical factory-derived documentation shows unusually broad propulsion choice:
- Volvo gasoline or diesel sterndrive;
- MerCruiser gasoline sterndrive;
- single or twin outboards.

B-Atlas therefore deliberately does **not** force one propulsion or fuel type across the RF-246 family.

The same source explicitly gives 70 Imperial gallons of fuel and 23 Imperial gallons of water, demonstrating the value of preserving source unit type before conversion.

## Architecture result

This batch reinforces the distinction between:
- historical named variant;
- current-generation redesign;
- shared-hull bridge/layout configuration;
- brand lineage;
- and propulsion configuration.

It also removes one previous data-quality concern: the C-Dory 26 Pro Angler should remain in B-Atlas as a legitimate historical model.
