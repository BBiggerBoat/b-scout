# B-Atlas v6.26.1 — Design Refinement Pass

## Visual refinement
- Reduced the difference between the largest and smallest type sizes and increased small/supporting text legibility.
- Added a medium-blue accent to the B-Atlas palette and applied it to key headings and progress indicators.
- Reworked Home entry cards: removed PLAN/DREAM pills, left-aligned labels, added clear card borders, and removed faint decorative rules.
- Reduced weight of descriptive text under Plan choices.
- Strengthened the Specific Waterways panel background and border while removing unnecessary internal item rules.
- Made primary Plan buttons higher contrast with white text.
- Constrained boat-family illustrations so they cannot overlap labels or adjacent choices.
- Removed engine icons and the decorative block beside Engine arrangement.
- Reduced Research result action-button size, switched their labels to white, and increased prominence of vessel/model names.
- Unified the Search button with the B-Atlas blue palette and removed the obsolete Hide Models Without Images control.

## Behaviour
- Header is now sticky and remains visible while navigating/scrolling.
- Reworked Previous/Next Plan navigation through one step-movement function and scrolls the active step header into view after movement.

## Deferred
- Canonical URLs, Schema.org, sitemap, IndexNow, Search Console and Bing migration remain intentionally unchanged for the later SEO/domain migration release.
