## Important qualification

“Completed” means each current manufacturer has received the first structured validation pass under Canonical Architecture v1.4. It does **not** mean every field is known or final.

B-Atlas deliberately retains:
- unresolved model years;
- conflicting source measurements;
- semi-custom configuration variability;
- hull-specific machinery differences;
- provisional low-volume model identities.

Those are data-quality states, not failures. Unknown information remains unknown rather than being guessed.
