Bayliner 3055 Ciera Sunbridge / 305 SB Express Cruiser

1999–2007

The later Bayliner 3055 Ciera Sunbridge is a wide-beam twin-sterndrive express cruiser focused on family accommodation and cockpit seating. It became the Bayliner 305 after the 3055 designation was dropped, so year-specific naming is important when comparing examples.

Best for

  • Family weekend cruising
  • Buyers prioritizing interior volume and cockpit seating
  • Owners comfortable maintaining twin gasoline sterndrives

Trade-offs

  • Twin gasoline sterndrives increase fuel and maintenance costs
  • No practical walkaround side decks
  • Value-oriented finish is less robust than premium cruisers
  • Width and weight reduce trailer and storage flexibility

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Scout’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Both sterndrives, transom assemblies and engine cooling systems
  • Fuel-system age and ventilation
  • Deck, transom and cockpit moisture
  • Electrical, sanitation and air-conditioning systems where fitted
