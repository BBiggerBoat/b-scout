Jeanneau Merry Fisher 1095 Fly Flybridge

From 2020

The Jeanneau Merry Fisher 1095 Fly / NC 1095 Fly is a French enclosed family cruiser from Jeanneau's Merry Fisher/NC family. It uses outboard propulsion and a planing hull, with emphasis on protected helm visibility, flexible cockpit space and compact weekend accommodation.

Best for

  • Weekend and short coastal cruising
  • Owners wanting enclosed outboard operation
  • Family day use with occasional overnighting

Trade-offs

  • Planing-speed fuel use is higher than displacement trawlers
  • Outboards occupy transom space and place machinery outside the hull
  • Tankage and storage are more weekend-oriented than long-range trawler layouts

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Atlas’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Inspect outboard service history, transom mounting and steering
  • Inspect pilothouse windows, side doors, roof hatches and deck penetrations
  • Inspect cockpit drains, sliding seating and exterior hardware
  • Verify actual tankage, sleeping layout and optional equipment
