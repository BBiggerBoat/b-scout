Fjord 40 Open Walkaround Day Cruiser

2007–2021

The Fjord 40 Open is the model that established Fjord’s modern walkaround dayboat identity. It combines a broad open deck, deep-V planing hull and twin Volvo Penta IPS diesels with a compact cabin for occasional overnight use.

Best for

  • Fast coastal day boating and entertaining
  • Owners valuing distinctive walkaround access
  • Occasional overnighting with emphasis on deck space

Trade-offs

  • Open layout sacrifices enclosed living volume and weather protection
  • Twin IPS systems increase service complexity and cost
  • Nearly 13-foot beam creates marina and storage implications

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Atlas’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Inspect IPS service history, seals, corrosion protection and steering
  • Inspect deck structure, teak and hardware bedding
  • Inspect engine-room ventilation, cooling and exhaust systems
  • Inspect cabin/head plumbing and electrical systems
  • Verify T-top and air-draft configuration
