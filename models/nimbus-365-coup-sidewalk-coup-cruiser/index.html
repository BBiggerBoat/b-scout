Nimbus 365 Coupé Sidewalk Coupé Cruiser

From 2011

The Nimbus 365 Coupé is a Swedish all-weather family cruiser built around an enclosed deck saloon, two cabins and shaft-drive diesel propulsion. Factory documentation shows both single- and twin-engine options, making exact machinery configuration important when comparing individual boats.

Best for

  • Couples or families wanting an all-weather Scandinavian cruiser
  • Extended inland and coastal cruising
  • Owners prioritizing enclosed helm comfort and practical side access

Trade-offs

  • Systems and accommodation are more complex than a compact single-cabin cruiser
  • Twin-engine examples increase maintenance burden and machinery-space crowding
  • Higher cruising speeds carry greater fuel use than displacement trawlers

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Atlas’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Confirm single- or twin-engine factory configuration
  • Inspect shaft seals, stern gear, cooling and exhaust systems
  • Inspect deck saloon windows, doors and roof openings for leakage
  • Review electrical, heating, hot-water and sanitation systems
  • Verify air draft with mast and antenna configuration
