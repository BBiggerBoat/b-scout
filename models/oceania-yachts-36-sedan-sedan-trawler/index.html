Oceania Yachts 36 Sedan Sedan Trawler

1980–1993

The Ocean Alexander 36 Sedan is an early Taiwan-built flybridge sedan cruiser associated with Alexander Marine's better-finished motoryacht tradition. The recorded layout emphasizes teak interior joinery, twin-diesel shaft propulsion and coastal cruising, but early small-model documentation is less complete than for later Ocean Alexander ranges.

Best for

  • Buyers seeking a traditional Northwest-style twin-diesel sedan
  • Coastal cruising couples wanting lower and upper helm positions
  • Owners willing to verify an uncommon early model carefully

Trade-offs

  • Early-model documentation and standard specifications are incomplete
  • Twin machinery and aging systems increase maintenance burden
  • Individual boats can differ materially after decades of refits

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Atlas’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Confirm exact model, year and builder plate before relying on marketplace naming
  • Inspect decks, windows, flybridge and rail penetrations for moisture
  • Inspect tanks, wiring, plumbing, generator and HVAC installations
  • Inspect twin diesels, exhaust, cooling, shafts and steering
  • Verify hull-specific air draft, displacement and tankage
