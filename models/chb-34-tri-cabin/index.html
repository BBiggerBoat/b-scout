CHB 34 Tri-Cabin

1977–1987

The CHB 34 is a tri-cabin trawler and semi-displacement produced from 1977 to 1987. Typical examples use single or twin inboard, diesel, and shaft. The recorded configuration includes a flybridge, an aft cabin, 2 cabins, 6 berths, and 2 heads.

Best for

  • Two-cabin accommodation in a compact, affordable classic-trawler platform

Trade-offs

  • Aft cabin increases windage and complicates access
  • specifications and construction vary among importers and yards

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Atlas’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Confirm HIN, yard, importer and actual layout
  • Moisture-map decks, cabin sides and flybridge
  • Inspect windows and exterior teak
  • Inspect steel or iron fuel tanks
  • Inspect exhaust, shaft, rudder and steering
  • Audit electrical and plumbing modifications
