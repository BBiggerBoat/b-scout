Marine Trader 36 Double Cabin Double Cabin

1975–1993

The Marine Trader 36 Double Cabin is a traditional aft-cabin flybridge cruiser built on a solid-fiberglass hull with hard aft chines and a long prop-protecting keel. Its teak interior provides separate forward and aft cabins, two heads and a lower helm. Single- and twin-diesel arrangements exist, and a 1977 layout revision changed the galley and aft-cabin arrangement.

Best for

  • Couples wanting private guest accommodation
  • Extended inland and coastal cruising at moderate speed
  • Buyers prepared to assess systems and construction carefully

Trade-offs

  • Exterior teak and aging deck or window structures require maintenance
  • Single- and twin-engine versions have different access and handling
  • Original tanks, wiring and plumbing may require renewal
  • Quality and details vary among production years and builders

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Atlas’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Moisture-test teak-deck fasteners, deck penetrations, window surrounds and cabin-house joints
  • Inspect fuel tanks, fill and vent hoses, supports and access for corrosion or leakage
  • Inspect original wiring, shore-power equipment, bonding and later electrical modifications
  • Inspect exhaust systems, engine mounts, shafting, rudders and steering gear
  • Confirm builder, model configuration and major refit history from hull documentation
