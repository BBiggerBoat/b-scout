Marine Trader 34 Sedan

1973–2001

The Marine Trader 34 is a sedan trawler and semi-displacement produced from 1973 to 2001. Typical examples use single inboard, diesel, and shaft. The recorded configuration includes a flybridge, 1 cabin, 4 berths, and 1 head.

Construction
Solid fiberglass hull; early superstructures were fiberglass over plywood, later boats used molded fiberglass; teak decks/window frames reduced or eliminated in later production

Best for

  • Widely available, economical classic trawler with practical walkaround decks and good interior volume

Trade-offs

  • Early plywood superstructures, teak decks and variable fit-and-finish can require major rehabilitation

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Atlas’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Confirm year and generation
  • Inspect early plywood superstructure and teak decks
  • Inspect windows and deck fittings
  • Inspect fuel tanks
  • Inspect Lehman/Cummins cooling and exhaust
  • Inspect shaft, rudder and steering
