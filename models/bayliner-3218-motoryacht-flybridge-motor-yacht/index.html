Bayliner 3218 Motoryacht Flybridge Motor Yacht

1987–1989

The Bayliner 3218 Motoryacht belongs to the 32xx family of compact flybridge cruisers and uses twin inboards, a lower helm and a two-stateroom interior to deliver unusually high accommodation volume for its length. Diesel Hino installations are common, although gasoline versions also exist.

Trade-offs

  • Machinery access is tight
  • Two engines double routine propulsion service points
  • Model naming overlaps with 3270/3288 family references
  • Individual boats may have gasoline or diesel power

Inspection focus

  • Fuel tanks, crossover plumbing and return routing
  • Window and deck moisture
