Fjord 36 Open Walkaround Day Cruiser

2011–2020

The Fjord 36 Open is a German-built Scandinavian-style walkaround day cruiser with a deep-V planing hull, large open deck and a compact overnight cabin. Its design prioritizes social deck space and high-speed coastal operation rather than traditional cruising volume.

Best for

  • Day boating and fast coastal cruising
  • Owners prioritizing outdoor entertaining and walkaround access
  • Occasional overnight use rather than extended liveaboard cruising

Trade-offs

  • Limited enclosed living space for extended cruising
  • Twin diesels and sterndrives add maintenance complexity
  • Open layout provides less weather protection than a coupé cruiser

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Scout’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Inspect sterndrive service history, bellows and corrosion protection
  • Inspect teak/deck surfaces and hardware bedding
  • Inspect engine cooling, exhaust and fuel systems
  • Inspect cabin/head ventilation and plumbing
  • Confirm T-top, bimini and air-draft configuration
