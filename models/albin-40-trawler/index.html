Albin 40 Trawler

1987–1993

The Albin 40 Trawler is a Taiwan-built semi-displacement aft-cabin cruiser with two staterooms, galley-down interior, lower helm, flybridge and full walkaround decks. A deep skeg protects the propellers and rudders. Single-diesel power existed, but twin Ford Lehman and later Cummins installations are common, giving the 40 greater maneuverability and moderate cruising speeds at the cost of more machinery.

Best for

  • Couples wanting a roomy traditional liveaboard cruiser
  • Extended inland and coastal cruising
  • Buyers accepting larger-boat maintenance for accommodation and deck space

Trade-offs

  • Exterior teak and aging systems create high maintenance burden
  • Beam, displacement and flybridge increase marina and haul-out costs
  • Twin-engine versions materially increase service and fuel costs
  • Traditional teak interior and multi-level layout will not suit every buyer

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Atlas’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Moisture-test teak decks, deck penetrations, window surrounds and cabin-house joints
  • Inspect fuel tanks, fill and vent hoses, supports and access
  • Inspect original and modified AC/DC electrical systems
  • Inspect exhaust, cooling, engine mounts, shafting, rudders and steering gear
  • Assess exterior teak condition and prior structural or deck repairs
