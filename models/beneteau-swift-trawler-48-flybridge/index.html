Beneteau Swift Trawler 48 Flybridge

From 2022

The Beneteau Swift Trawler 48 is a modern three-cabin flybridge passagemaker-style cruiser with twin shaft-drive Cummins diesels and a semi-planing hull. Factory data emphasizes long-range operation at displacement speeds while retaining cruising speeds around the high teens.

Best for

  • Extended family coastal and Great Lakes cruising
  • Owners wanting modern passagemaker comfort with speed reserve
  • Buyers with marina access for a 48-foot yacht

Trade-offs

  • 19-foot bridge clearance is a major inland-route constraint
  • Twin diesel and yacht-scale systems raise maintenance cost
  • 14-foot-9-inch beam requires substantial marina infrastructure

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Scout’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Inspect twin Cummins engines, shafts, cooling and exhaust
  • Inspect flybridge structure, T-top options and exposed systems
  • Inspect side door, glazing and roof penetrations
  • Review generator, battery, charging, sanitation and domestic systems
