Marine Trader 38 Double Cabin Double Cabin

1980–2000

The Marine Trader 38 Double Cabin is a two-stateroom aft-cabin flybridge cruiser with a solid-fiberglass hull, long keel and traditional teak interior. Galley-up and galley-down arrangements were offered, and the aft cabin has direct cockpit access on many examples. Propulsion ranges from economical single-diesel installations to faster twin-diesel versions.

Best for

  • Couples wanting a roomy aft-cabin cruiser
  • Long-duration inland and coastal cruising
  • Buyers comparing layout and engine configuration carefully

Trade-offs

  • Galley and propulsion variations materially change use and service access
  • Teak decks and exterior joinery require inspection and upkeep
  • Later higher-power twin versions increase complexity and fuel consumption
  • Quality and systems condition vary widely among individual boats

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Atlas’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Moisture-test teak-deck fasteners, deck penetrations, window surrounds and cabin-house joints
  • Inspect fuel tanks, fill and vent hoses, supports and access for corrosion or leakage
  • Inspect original wiring, shore-power equipment, bonding and later electrical modifications
  • Inspect exhaust systems, engine mounts, shafting, rudders and steering gear
  • Confirm builder, model configuration and major refit history from hull documentation
