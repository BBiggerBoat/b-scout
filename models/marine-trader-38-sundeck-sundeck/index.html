Marine Trader 38 Sundeck Sundeck

1985–1998

The Marine Trader 38 Sundeck is an aft-cabin flybridge cruiser with an elevated covered aft deck, two private cabins and twin-diesel propulsion in typical examples. The sundeck arrangement creates more sheltered outdoor living space than the traditional double-cabin profile, at the cost of greater height and windage.

Best for

  • Couples prioritizing aft-deck and liveaboard space
  • Extended inland and coastal cruising where height is acceptable
  • Buyers comfortable verifying exact model identity

Trade-offs

  • Higher profile increases windage and air-draft restrictions
  • More exterior structure and enclosure create additional leak paths
  • Twin engines and larger systems increase operating cost
  • The database record may overlap marketplace naming for other 38-foot configurations

Known concerns

  • No model-specific concern has been promoted to B-Atlas’s evidence threshold. This does not mean the model has no age-, condition- or hull-specific risks.

Inspection focus

  • Moisture-test teak-deck fasteners, deck penetrations, window surrounds and cabin-house joints
  • Inspect fuel tanks, fill and vent hoses, supports and access for corrosion or leakage
  • Inspect original wiring, shore-power equipment, bonding and later electrical modifications
  • Inspect exhaust systems, engine mounts, shafting, rudders and steering gear
  • Confirm builder, model configuration and major refit history from hull documentation
