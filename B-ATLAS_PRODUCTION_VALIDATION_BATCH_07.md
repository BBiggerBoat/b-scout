# B-Atlas Production Validation Batch 07 — Back Cove + Sabre + Duffy + Fortier + Hunt Yachts

**Release:** v6.37.0  
**Canonical architecture:** v1.4  
**Scope:** 21 current B-Atlas records.

## Back Cove

Back Cove provides unusually strong manufacturer/heritage documentation.

The Back Cove 29 contains a source-quality issue worth preserving: the current heritage page displays 9'5" beam but also gives 3.20 m, while the original factory brochure gives 10'5". Since 3.20 m corresponds to approximately 10'6", the 9'5" value is treated as a source typo and the original brochure value is canonical.

The Back Cove 33 was explicitly relaunched as the Back Cove 34 in 2012. They remain separately searchable because those are the market names users encounter, but the lineage is now recorded.

The 39O remains a separate high-performance outboard platform rather than being normalized like the single-diesel inboard Back Cove line.

## Sabre

The 34 Hard Top Express receives stronger manufacturer dimensions and a separate-stall-shower fact.

The 38 Salon Express now uses production phases:
- original generation: pod drive;
- 2026 reintroduction: Volvo Penta DPI sterndrives.

This is exactly the kind of propulsion evolution that should not be flattened into one universal value.

## Duffy

Duffy is fundamentally different from tightly standardized production cruisers.

Many Duffy hulls were completed semi-custom for commercial or recreational use, and Atlantic Boat still treats the boats as hull families/kits. B-Atlas therefore canonicalizes stable hull facts while avoiding universal claims about tankage, cabin count or machinery where those depend on the specific finish.

The Duffy 35 is strongly documented as a Spencer Lincoln design from 1981 with production beginning in 1982. The Duffy 42 is documented through 2006.

The Duffy 29 and 39 remain deliberately conservative because strong model-wide universal specifications were not found in this pass.

## Fortier

The Fortier 26 is strongly documented by the builder: 26.9 ft LOA, 10 ft beam, 30 in draft, 6,500 lb displacement, single Volvo diesel and full molded keel/skeg protection.

The Fortier 33 supports either standard twin diesel inboards or optional Volvo IPS. Propulsion is therefore not forced into a single model-wide code.

## Hunt Yachts

The Surfhunter 29 has unusually broad factory propulsion flexibility: sterndrive, V-drive, waterjet and outboard arrangements were all documented. B-Atlas leaves propulsion configuration-specific.

The Surfhunter 36 Coupe is now validated at 36'6" × 11'4" × 3'3", 14,000 lb. Standard power used a Cummins/ZF pod arrangement, but inboard alternatives were offered. It also has a genuine separate stall shower.

## Architecture result

This batch strengthens B-Atlas's handling of:
- manufacturer heritage vs brochure conflicts;
- renamed model lineage;
- semi-custom hull families;
- and propulsion configurations that cannot safely be expressed as a single model-wide value.
