# B-Atlas Production Validation Batch 05 — Cape Dory + Cheoy Lee + Gulfstar + Californian

**Release:** v6.35.0  
**Canonical architecture:** v1.4  
**Scope:** 17 current B-Atlas records.

## Cape Dory

The Cape Dory 28 family is treated as one proven hull with separate Cruiser, Flybridge and Open Fisherman variants.

The brochure-derived Cape Dory Owners Association specification gives **2 ft 11 in draft**. HMY lists 2 ft 9 in for the Flybridge/Open. B-Atlas now uses 2 ft 11 in canonically and keeps the 2 ft 9 in figures as conflicting secondary evidence rather than averaging or silently discarding them.

The 30, 33 and 36 powerboats are correctly treated as modified-V / planing-capable Down East cruisers rather than displacement trawlers. Their full-length/protective keels remain separate from hull-behaviour classification.

The Cape Dory 40 Explorer is corrected to 1992–94. Hull #1 was Cape Dory-built; later boats were built by Newport Shipyards after acquisition of the name/molds. That is builder succession inside one model identity, not a new model.

## Cheoy Lee

Original-brochure material available through the Cheoy Lee association gives strong dimensions for the 28, 32 and 35 Trawlers.

The broad B-Atlas 36 Trawler record is handled more cautiously. A brochure specifically documents the **36 MKII** at 36'6" × 13' × 3'6". Those dimensions are retained as MKII evidence/alias data and are not falsely applied to every 36 Trawler in the broad 1968–79 record.

## Gulfstar

The 36 and 43 Trawlers are particularly useful lineage cases because both were derived from Gulfstar motorsailer hulls.

Both also had **1975 MK II** changes:
- 36 MK II: raised aft deck, full-beam master, larger flybridge, bigger rudders, revised interior;
- 43 MK II: sundeck layout, revised flybridge, teak interior, larger master.

These are production phases, not separate base models.

The 44 Motor Cruiser is now correctly classified as **full displacement** even though it looks like a conventional motor yacht.

## Californian

The 34 and 38 LRCs highlight the importance of separating visual family/style from hull behaviour.

Both have trawler styling, but HMY describes solid-fiberglass **modified-V hulls capable of planing-speed performance**. They therefore remain in the trawler discovery family while their canonical hull behaviour is planing-capable.

This prevents B-Atlas from treating every trawler-looking boat as a displacement or semi-displacement hull.
