# B-Atlas Lifecycle Structure

Updated: 2026-08-07

## 1. Plan
Introspective discovery and education before search.
Guided questions define intended cruising, size, hull behaviour, propulsion, features and crew needs.

## 2. Dream
Broad model discovery.
The standard search translates the plan into possible boats. Emphasis is on appeal, quick model character, images and walkthrough videos.

## 3. Research
Deep permanent-model knowledge.
Model character, design lineage, specifications, strengths, trade-offs, known problems, ownership characteristics, evidence and confidence.

## 4. Buy
Individual boats for sale.
Listings, saved candidates, seller conversations, inspections, surveys, repair estimates, price context, offers and acquisition decisions.

## 5. Own
Individual boat record plus owner resources.
Manuals, documents, clubs, associations, forums, technical videos and owner knowledge.

## 6. Sell
Prepare an owned boat for buyer scrutiny.
Maintenance records, upgrades, documents, disclosures, unresolved concerns and future valuation/listing preparation.

## Compatibility
Existing internal keys are preserved where practical:
- dreaming = Dream
- research = new deep-research workspace
- buying = Buy
- owning = Own
- selling = Sell
- notebook remains a utility, not a lifecycle stage

Legacy navigation actions remain supported.
