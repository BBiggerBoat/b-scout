# Phase 8C-16 — Sail-Derived Motorsailers and Crossover Cruisers

Date: 2026-08-07

## Scope

Normalized 15 existing records across Nauticat, Fisher, Island Packet, Gozzard, Corbin, Seawind, Shannon, Gemini and PDQ.

## Main identity/classification findings

- Gozzard Pilgrim 40 is a full-displacement power motor yacht/trawler, not a motorsailer.
- Corbin 39 is a pilothouse sailing cruiser with substantial owner-completed variation.
- Gemini 105Mc and Legacy 35 are sailing catamarans, not generic motorsailers; neither has a flybridge.
- PDQ 34 is a power catamaran; PDQ 36 is a sailing catamaran with materially different auxiliary arrangements by version.
- Seawind 1160 is a sailing catamaran lineage; the original 1160, Lite, Resort and later XL derivatives should not share specifications blindly.
- Island Packet SP Cruiser is a true motorsailer with a protected pilothouse, 110 hp diesel and simplified sailing rig; it is not a flybridge boat.
- Fisher and Nauticat records were corrected to pilothouse motorsailer configurations with no flybridges.
