# Community Contributions — Phase 10 Guide Integration

## Purpose
Reviewed community evidence now improves the permanent Guide without creating a separate social/community destination.

## Guide placement
- Research: normalized owner knowledge and approved owner photos, only when present.
- Buy: normalized problem/weakness and buyer-inspection knowledge, only when present.
- Owner Resources: approved manuals, documents and useful resources supplement curated resources.

## Presentation rules
- No empty community sections.
- The primary display is a concise normalized B-Scout summary, not a feed of raw posts.
- Evidence counts and conflict/applicability notes sit behind progressive disclosure.
- Conflicting evidence is shown as conflict, not averaged into consensus.
- Community report counts are evidence counts, not votes or contributor reputation.

## Static-prototype limitation
The live Guide can read reviewed records and attachments that exist in the same browser as the moderator console. The static JSON knowledge files are also supported for future publication. Cross-device community submissions still require a shared backend/service.
