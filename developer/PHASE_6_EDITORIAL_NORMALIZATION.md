# Phase 6 — Editorial Normalization

Phase 6 normalizes the authoritative `boatmodels.json` records without inventing unsupported model knowledge.

## Applied

- Neutral Overview created for all 287 models from recorded facts.
- Existing structured boat intelligence migrated for 34 models.
- Strengths, Trade-offs and Avoid If normalized as arrays.
- Suitability separated from Best For.
- Inspection Focus and Buyer Questions populated only from structured model intelligence or reviewed manufacturer-family guidance.
- Known Concerns left empty unless model-specific evidence satisfies the frozen definition.
- Owner Actions and Model Variations left empty where unsupported.
- EvidenceSummary populated with scoped evidence, coverage and unresolved information.
- Identity and dimension values reconciled against preferred internal facts; conflicts preserved for review.

## Explicit restraint

Legacy TypicalMission and CommonProblems remain available for migration review but were not automatically promoted. Generic age-related risks were not presented as model-specific defects.
