# B-Atlas Community Contributions — Phase 9

## Conflict and uncertainty handling

Phase 9 preserves disagreement rather than forcing consensus. Evidence attached to a normalized Knowledge Item is classified as **supports**, **contradicts**, or **qualifies**.

Knowledge Items can be marked: Confirmed, Community-supported, Limited evidence, Conflicting information, or Applicability uncertain.

Applicability is independently recorded as known, probable, uncertain, or conflicting, with optional year range and variants.

Any contradictory evidence automatically forces the effective Knowledge State to `conflicting_information` during evidence-summary refresh. Contradictory reports are not deleted and are not counted as support.

Public wording remains conservative. Phase 9 does not publish these items into Guides and does not change canonical model data.
