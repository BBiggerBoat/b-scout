# B-Atlas Production Validation Batch 11 — Prairie + Tollycraft + Uniflite + Trojan + Ocean Alexander + Nordhavn + SeaPiper + Seahorse

**Release:** v6.41.0  
**Canonical architecture:** v1.4

## Prairie
Prairie 29 and 36 receive stronger historical/technical normalization. The 36 is directly linked to the Atlantic 37 Double Cabin successor after Prairie closed and Atlantic acquired the tooling.

## Tollycraft
The 34 Sport Sedan and 37 Sedan are modified-V, planing-capable flybridge cruisers. Their previous semi-displacement classification was too conservative. Gasoline and diesel installations both exist, so fuel remains configuration-specific.

## Uniflite
The 36 Double Cabin remains one model line, but the 1984 36 II receives its own production phase because it introduced a fully cored hull, revised flybridge and interior, and materially lower weight.

## Trojan
Trojan's own numbering documentation confirms model 363 as the F-36 Tri Cabin. This reinforces B-Atlas's earlier architecture rule: where the builder explicitly changes model identity/designation—as Trojan did with its F-series—the fiberglass boat should remain a separate canonical identity rather than merely a material phase of an older wood boat.

## Ocean Alexander
The 39 Sedan remains conservative. Surviving examples often have substantial swim-grid/hull extensions, so listing LOA/weight can describe the modified individual boat rather than the factory model. Beam/draft are strengthened; overall length and displacement are left unresolved model-wide.

## Nordhavn
The Nordhavn 40 is treated as one continuous hull lineage with an N40II production generation beginning in 2004. Nordhavn states the fundamental scantlings/hull remained the same while windows, rubrail, yard and machinery evolved. Draft and engine output are therefore phase-scoped.

## SeaPiper
The 35 receives factory-backed dimensions, 16,500 lb design displacement, 85 hp Beta diesel, and explicit metric tank capacities.

## Seahorse
Coot 35/38 are normalized as steel, full-displacement pocket trawlers. Coot 38 published displacement figures conflict materially, so B-Atlas does not invent a compromise scalar.

## Architecture result
This batch validates all three identity mechanisms:
- explicit manufacturer model identity (Trojan F-series),
- production phase inside a continuous hull (Nordhavn 40 / N40II, Uniflite 36 II),
- successor lineage across manufacturer/brand changes (Prairie 36 → Atlantic 37).
